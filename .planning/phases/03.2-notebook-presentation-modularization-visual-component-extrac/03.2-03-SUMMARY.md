# Phase 03.2 Plan 03 Summary: Core Quaternary Engine Rigor & Vocabulary Standardization

## Executive Summary
Executed Plan `03.2-03` to refactor the core quaternary computational session and data models. Eliminated all synthetic benchmarks, removed static `w_pisa = 3.0` assignments and hardcoded `is_groel` / `is_insulin` synthesis shortcuts in `quaternary/session.py`. Implemented assembly-aware PDB candidate ranking in Stage 2 that penalizes isolated domain fragments (e.g. `1DK7`) over full multimeric biological assemblies (`1AON`, `1GRL`), extracted genuine PISA interfaces, biologically disambiguated GroEL self-assembly ($A_{14}$) from GroEL-GroES ($A_{14}B_7$), standardized consensus statuses and database audit query states, and implemented strict novel sequence fallbacks.

## Key Changes & Implementations

### 1. Data Models & Vocabulary Standardization
- **`quaternary/models/stoichiometry.py`**:
  - Expanded `EvidenceStatus` enum: `CONFIRMED`, `SUPPORTED`, `CONFLICTING`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `PREDICTED`, `UNKNOWN`.
  - Added `QueryState` enum: `NOT_QUERIED`, `QUERY_SUCCESS_NO_HIT`, `QUERY_SUCCESS_WITH_HIT`, `QUERY_FAILED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `SKIPPED`, `PREDICTION_NOT_EXECUTED`.
  - Added `DatabaseAuditRecord` model capturing database identifier, query state, hit count, latency ms, provenance, and contextual details.
  - Updated `EvidenceCoverage` to hold a list of `DatabaseAuditRecord` items and `database_query_states` mapping.
  - Segregated `InferredFunction` fields: `observed_function`, `predicted_function`, `quaternary_mechanism_hypothesis`, `confidence`, and `supporting_evidence`.
- **`quaternary/models/provenance.py`**:
  - Added `ProvenanceSource` enum (`EXPERIMENTAL`, `DATABASE-DERIVED`, `PREDICTED`, `HEURISTIC`, `HARDCODED_BENCHMARK`).
- **`quaternary/models/session_state.py`**:
  - Extended `QuaternaryStructureOutput` with `experimental_oligomeric_state`, `predicted_oligomeric_state`, and `fallback_representation`.
- **`quaternary/function_inference.py`**:
  - Updated `FunctionInferenceEngine.infer_function` to populate segregated `InferredFunction` fields.

### 2. Elimination of Hardcoding & Rigorous Engine Refactoring (`quaternary/session.py`)
- **Stage 2 (Candidate Search & Ranking)**:
  - Completely removed the `known_benchmarks` dictionary and static `w_pisa = 3.0` override.
  - Implemented dynamic Assembly-Aware Candidate Ranking: evaluates coverage, resolution, and macromolecular assembly subunit count, applying domain fragment penalties for candidates with $<50\%$ sequence coverage or monomeric states when higher-order multimers exist.
- **Stage 3 (Thermodynamic Qualification & PISA)**:
  - Nuanced thermodynamic stability classifications into `STABLE`, `INTERMEDIATE`, `DISSOCIATED`, and `UNKNOWN` based on complex assembly size, solvation $\Delta^i G$, and stabilizing interfaces without simplistic $\Delta G \le 0$ lattice artifact assumptions.
  - Extracted authentic interface contacts without synthetic placeholders.
- **Stage 6 (Synthesis & Functional Disambiguation)**:
  - Completely eliminated `is_groel` and `is_insulin` hardcoded synthesis branches.
  - Dynamic deduction from UniProt subunit text, PDB assemblies, PISA interfaces, and Complex Portal records.
  - GroEL correctly resolves to $A_{14}$ homo-tetradecamer (`D7` symmetry) for self-assembly, with GroEL-GroES modeled as an $A_{14}B_7$ functional heterocomplex.
  - Insulin dynamically accounts for monomer/dimer/hexamer equilibrium based on PDB and UniProt records.
  - Novel sequences strictly output `Experimental=UNKNOWN`, `Predicted=UNKNOWN`, `Fallback='MONOMER BASELINE'`, and `Status='INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE'`.
  - AlphaFold-Multimer explicitly recorded as `PREDICTION_NOT_EXECUTED` (`External prediction runner not configured`).

### 3. Client & Offline Fixtures Grounding
- **`quaternary/clients/base.py`**:
  - Refactored `get_with_cache` to check for query-specific fixture files first, then cache, then generic samples in offline mode, ensuring accurate data retrieval for real benchmark entries (`1AON`, `1GRL`, `1DK7`, `4INS`).
- **`quaternary/fixtures/`**:

  - Added monomer boundary disclaimer to `get_structural_context`.
