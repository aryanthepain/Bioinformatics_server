1. In `quaternary/session.py`:
   - Remove `known_benchmarks` dictionary and fake `w_pisa = 3.0` assignment in Stage 2.
   - Implement Assembly-Aware Candidate Scoring in Stage 2:
     - For each candidate from RCSB, inspect macromolecular assembly size, subunit count, resolution, and sequence coverage.
     - Rank candidates giving weight to biological relevance (full-length multimer matching UniProt sequence length vs isolated monomeric domain fragments like `1DK7`).
     - Pick the best assembly candidate (e.g. `1OEL` or `1AON` for GroEL).
   - In Stage 3 (PISA):
     - Query PISA for the ranked candidate, extract actual interface contacts, buried surface area, $\Delta^i G$, H-bonds, salt bridges, and contact residues.
     - Nuance PISA assessment into `STABLE`, `DISSOCIATED`, `INTERMEDIATE`, `UNKNOWN` without defaulting to lattice artifact solely on $\Delta G \le 0$.
   - In Stage 6 (Synthesis):
     - Eliminate hardcoded `elif is_groel:` and `elif is_insulin:` shortcuts!
     - Synthesize stoichiometry dynamically from actual Stage 1 (UniProt/Complex Portal), Stage 2 (PDB assemblies), Stage 3 (PISA validation), and Stage 4 (SWISS-MODEL).
     - For GroEL (`P0A6F5`), deduce `A14` homooligomer for `target_self_assembly` from PDB/PISA/UniProt, and assign `A14B7` to `functional_complexes` via Complex Portal / UniProt annotation.
     - For novel sequences, set experimental state to `UNKNOWN`, predicted to `UNKNOWN`, fallback to `MONOMER BASELINE`, and status to `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`.
     - Label AlphaFold-Multimer as `PREDICTION_NOT_EXECUTED` (External prediction runner not configured).
