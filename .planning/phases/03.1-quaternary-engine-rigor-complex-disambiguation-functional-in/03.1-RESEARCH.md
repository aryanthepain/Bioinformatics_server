# Phase 03.1: Quaternary Engine Rigor & Functional Inference - Research & Technical Architecture

## 1. Thermodynamic Metrics & Sign Conventions (PISA)

### PDBe PISA Energy Definitions
- **Interface Solvation Free Energy ($\Delta^i G$ or $\Delta G_{\text{int}}$)**:
  - Calculated as the difference between the solvation energy of the complex and that of isolated subunits upon interface formation.
  - **Sign Convention**: $\Delta^i G < 0$ (negative) indicates a favorable, hydrophobic interface that gains stability upon burial of non-polar surface area.
  - Typical stable biological interfaces: $\Delta^i G \in [-5.0, -35.0]\text{ kcal/mol}$.
- **Assembly Dissociation Free Energy ($\Delta G_{\text{diss}}$)**:
  - Free energy required to dissociate the assembly into isolated monomers in solution.
  - **Sign Convention**: $\Delta G_{\text{diss}} > 0$ (positive) indicates a thermodynamically stable assembly that requires energy input to dissociate.
  - Typical stable oligomers: $\Delta G_{\text{diss}} > 0$ (e.g. GroEL tetradecamer $> 20\text{ kcal/mol}$).
- **Interface Buried Surface Area (BSA)**:
  - Total surface area buried upon interface formation ($\text{Å}^2$). Biological interfaces typically have $\text{BSA} > 800 - 1000\text{ Å}^2$ per interface.

## 2. Biological Assembly Disambiguation & Entity Mapping

### RCSB PDB Entity vs Biological Assembly Architecture
1. **PDB Entry Level**: Contains entities (polymers, non-polymers, water).
   - Entity 1: Protein chain(s) (e.g., GroEL, UniProt `P0A6F5`).
   - Entity 2: Partner protein chain(s) (e.g., GroES, UniProt `P0A6F9`).
   - Entity 3: Ligands/ions (e.g., ADP, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$).
2. **Biological Assembly (PDB Assembly ID)**:
   - Built by applying crystallographic symmetry operators to asymmetric unit chains.
   - For PDB `1AON` (GroEL–GroES–ADP complex):
     - GroEL chains: A, B, C, D, E, F, G (Ring 1) + H, I, J, K, L, M, N (Ring 2) = 14 copies.
     - GroES chains: O, P, Q, R, S, T, U (Dome cap) = 7 copies.
     - Assembly Stoichiometry: $A_{14}B_7$.
3. **Disambiguation Rule**:
   - `target_self_assembly`: Filter assembly chains matching the target protein entity/UniProt accession only $\rightarrow$ `A14` (GroEL homomer).
   - `functional_complexes[]`: Assemblies containing target entity + partner entities $\rightarrow$ `A14B7` (GroEL–GroES functional complex).
   - `observed_assemblies[]`: Record all distinct assemblies observed across experimental PDB entries with conditions (e.g., Insulin in PDB `4INS` = Hexamer $A_6$ with $\text{Zn}^{2+}$, PDB `2INS` = Dimer $A_2$, Monomer $A_1$).

## 3. Absence of Evidence vs Monomer

### Biological Evidence Hierarchy
When analyzing a novel sequence:
- AlphaFold DB or single-chain modeling provides **monomeric tertiary coordinate context**, NOT experimental evidence that the protein exists in nature as a monomer!
- Labeling absence of multimeric evidence as `MONOMER` is scientifically false.
- **Evidence Status Taxonomy**:
  - `CONFIRMED`: Multiple concordant experimental sources (PDB assembly + PISA + UniProt/Complex Portal).
  - `SUPPORTED`: Homology models (SWISS-MODEL $\text{GMQE} > 0.7$) or concordant physical interaction data (IntAct).
  - `PREDICTED`: Multimer simulation/docking prediction without direct experimental structure.
  - `UNCHARACTERIZED`: No experimental or homology quaternary evidence identified; single-chain structure prediction available.
  - `CONFLICTING`: Contradictory evidence between sources (e.g. crystal packaging vs solution monomer, or experimental $A_{14}$ vs predicted $A_2$).
  - `NO_EVIDENCE`: Target completely uncharacterized across all databases.

## 4. Quaternary-to-Function Inference Architecture

### Biological Role Determination from Quaternary Arrangement
Quaternary structure is an evolutionary adaptation that directly drives cellular function:
1. **Oligomeric Cavities & Enclosed Chambers**:
   - Stoichiometry $A_{14}$ / Two stacked heptameric rings ($D_7$ symmetry) $\rightarrow$ Molecular Chaperonin (assisted protein folding, preventing aggregation in shielded hydrophobic chamber).
2. **Toroidal & Hexameric Rings ($C_6$ / $D_3$)**:
   - Central channel / pore $\rightarrow$ Ring helicase, unfoldase, AAA+ ATPase, or transmembrane pore.
3. **Symmetric Dimers / Tetramers ($C_2$ / $D_2$)**:
   - Two-fold rotational symmetry with basic contact patches $\rightarrow$ DNA-binding transcription factor (e.g., bZIP, helix-turn-helix), or allosterically regulated enzyme (active sites formed at subunit interfaces).
4. **Partner-Containing Functional Complexes**:
   - Association with specific partner subunits (e.g. GroES co-chaperonin cap, ribosome subunits, proteasome regulatory particles) $\rightarrow$ Informs exact pathway and mechanism.

### Data Ingestion for Functional Inference
- **UniProtKB API**: Parse `comments(type=FUNCTION)`, `comments(type=CATALYTIC_ACTIVITY)`, `dbReferences(type=GO)`, `dbReferences(type=InterPro)`.
- **IntAct API**: Map interactors to known cellular complexes and biological processes.
- **SWISS-MODEL & PDB Homologs**: Extract functional annotations from high-coverage structural templates.
- **Synthesis**: Produce an `InferredFunction` record containing:
  - `molecular_function`: Primary biochemical activity.
  - `biological_process`: Cellular pathway.
  - `oligomeric_mechanism_hypothesis`: Scientific deduction explaining why the quaternary structure is required for function.
  - `functional_homologs`: Top homologous complexes donating functional annotation.
  - `confidence`: Confidence level of the functional transfer.
