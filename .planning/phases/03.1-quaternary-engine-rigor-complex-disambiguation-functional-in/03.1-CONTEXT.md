# Phase 03.1: Quaternary Engine Rigor, Complex Disambiguation & Functional Inference - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 03.1 resolves fundamental biological modeling, scientific auditability, engineering rigor, and canonical stage alignment issues across the Quaternary Structure Engine and interactive notebook.

Crucially, it **recovers the project's original core mission**: given a protein sequence, use quaternary arrangement, oligomeric interfaces, partner complexes, and structural homologs to infer the biological role, molecular function, and quaternary-dependent mechanism of the target protein.

Key deliverables:
1. **Biological Reality & Disambiguation**: Segregate target self-assembly (e.g. GroEL A14 homomer) from functional partner complexes (e.g. GroEL–GroES A14B7 heteromer). Model multiple experimental assemblies and dynamic equilibria (e.g. Insulin A6 ⇌ A2 ⇌ A1).
2. **Absence of Evidence != Monomer**: Replace default monomer fallback for novel/unannotated sequences with explicit statuses (`UNCHARACTERIZED`, `NO_EVIDENCE_FOUND`, `PREDICTIVE_ONLY`, `SUPPORTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `CONFLICTING`). Never assert monomer without positive evidence.
3. **Real-Execution Only (Zero Simulation)**: Mandate real execution only for Stage 5. If live GPU or external multimer endpoint (e.g. ColabFold, ESMFold) is unavailable, emit truthful `StageStatus.UNAVAILABLE` with clear diagnostic reason. Never emit fake, mock, or synthetic numbers.
4. **PDB Entity Mapping & PISA Thermodynamic Rigor**: Map PDB entities to UniProt target accessions, chains, and stoichiometry. Keep PISA dissociation free energy ($\Delta G_{\text{diss}}$, positive = stable) distinct from interface solvation free energy ($\Delta^i G$, negative = favorable) with documented sign conventions. Flag crystal packing artifacts when $\Delta G_{\text{diss}} \le 0$.
5. **Canonical 1–6 Stage Architecture**: Realign the 6 stages uniformly across architecture documentation, `session.py`, and `Quaternary_Structure.ipynb`, eliminating all confusing method aliases.
6. **Quaternary-to-Function Inference Engine**: Extract Gene Ontology (GO) terms (Molecular Function, Biological Process), UniProt catalytic activities, Pfam/InterPro domains from target and homologous complexes, and IntAct partner pathways. Synthesize an `InferredFunction` report connecting quaternary stoichiometry and symmetry to biological mechanism using a hybrid rule engine and extensible archetype registry.
7. **Complete Auditability & Dynamic Notebook Presentation**: Rich provenance records, Component Identity Table, Interface Contact Residue Table, Search Coverage & Gaps report, and 100% dynamic metric rendering in the notebook without hardcoded markdown numbers.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Quaternary-to-Function Inference Engine
- **D-01:** Hybrid Rule Engine with Extensible Archetype Registry — In `quaternary/function_inference.py`, map oligomeric geometry (stoichiometry, symmetry, interface size) to mechanistic archetypes (e.g. D7 ring -> chaperone folding cavity; C2/D2 -> allosteric regulation & cooperativity; ring hexamer -> helicase/pore/unfoldase; dimer -> DNA-binding bZIP). Combine with UniProt/GO/Pfam extraction and expose an extensible registry for custom quaternary mechanisms. — **Reversibility:** costly — core inference contract
- **D-02:** Multi-Factor Tiered Confidence Scoring — Compute an objective confidence score (0.0 to 1.0) combining evidence tier (PDB experimental > homology > simulated), interface thermodynamic stability (PISA $\Delta^i G$ and BSA), and GO term concordance. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-03:** Two-Stage Homolog & Partner Pathway Enrichment — Query target UniProt GO terms and catalytic comments; enrich with functional annotations from top structural homologs (SWISS-MODEL) and place into biological pathways via IntAct interactome partners. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-04:** Explicit UNCHARACTERIZED Status for Novel/Unannotated Sequences — When a target sequence lacks experimental PDB or homology evidence, report status `UNCHARACTERIZED`, extract any detected Pfam/InterPro domains, and label any oligomeric hypothesis as `PREDICTIVE_ONLY` with low confidence (<0.3). Never assert monomer. — **Reversibility:** reversible

### Complex Disambiguation & Dynamic Equilibria
- **D-05:** Multi-Layered Biological Quaternary Model — In `quaternary/models/stoichiometry.py` and `quaternary/models/session_state.py`, separate `target_self_assembly` (`TargetStoichiometry`, e.g. A14 / HOMOMER) from `functional_complexes` (`List[FunctionalComplex]`, e.g. GroEL–GroES A14B7 with partner accession P0A6F9 and stoichiometry B7). — **Reversibility:** costly — impacts pipeline data structures
- **D-06:** Multiple Observed Assemblies & Dynamic Equilibrium — Support `observed_assemblies: List[ObservedAssembly]` capturing distinct PDB assemblies under varying biological conditions (e.g. Insulin in zinc-stabilized hexamer storage form A6 vs active dimeric A2 and monomeric A1) with an explicit `primary_physiological_state`. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-07:** Strict 6-State Biological Status Enum — Replace boolean or forced monomer states with an expressive enum: `CONFIRMED` (PDB+PISA verified), `SUPPORTED` (homology/PDB without PISA), `PREDICTED` (prediction only), `UNCHARACTERIZED` (novel/no quaternary data), `CONFLICTING` (evidence disagreement), `NO_EVIDENCE` (database queries empty). — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-08:** Strict RCSB Entity-to-UniProt Mapping — Match entity reference sequence identifiers against target UniProt accession. Identify target chains, classify other polymer entities as partner proteins with their own UniProt IDs, and separate non-polymer cofactors and crystallization agents. — **Reversibility:** costly — touches PDB parsing logic

### Multimer Execution & Conflict Resolution
- **D-09:** Real Execution Only (No Fake Simulation) — Mandate real execution only for Stage 5. If live GPU or external multimer endpoint (e.g. ColabFold, ESMFold API) is unavailable or unconfigured, return `StageStatus.UNAVAILABLE` with a clear diagnostic message. Never fabricate mock metrics or synthetic simulation values. — **Reversibility:** costly — removes synthetic proxy fallback
- **D-10:** Explicit Conflict Audit Trail — Record `conflicts[]` containing `source_a` vs `source_b`, `stoichiometry_a` vs `stoichiometry_b`, `discrepancy_type`, and `resolution_rationale`, giving strict precedence to experimental crystal/cryo-EM evidence. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-11:** Distinct Thermodynamic Fields & Sign Rigor — Clearly document and separate $\Delta G_{\text{diss}}$ (>0 stable) from $\Delta^i G$ (<0 favorable). Flag assemblies with $\Delta G_{\text{diss}} \le 0$ as `POTENTIAL_CRYSTAL_PACKING_ARTIFACT`, and record a conflict if PDB author claims multimer but PISA indicates monomer/unstable. — **Reversibility:** reversible

### Notebook Telemetry & Auditable Tables
- **D-12:** 100% Dynamic Python/HTML Execution — All summary metrics, stoichiometry cards, and evidence badges are dynamically rendered by code cells from the live `SessionResult` object (using `IPython.display.HTML` / styled DataFrames). Markdown cells contain only conceptual explanations, eliminating hardcoded numbers. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-13:** Dual Styled Tables — Render (1) Component Identity Table (Protein, UniProt, Subunit Copies, PDB Chains, Biological Role) and (2) Interface Contact Table (Chain Pair, BSA, $\Delta^i G$, H-bonds, Salt bridges, Key Residues) as styled DataFrames/HTML tables. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-14:** Evidence Coverage & Gaps Dashboard — Render a structured audit table listing each Database/Stage, Execution Status, Hits Found, and explicit Gaps/Uncertainties identified during the query. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-15:** Executive Bio-Mechanistic Card + Evidence Breakdown — Styled HTML executive summary card displaying Inferred Function, Quaternary Mechanism Hypothesis (symmetry & interface role), Confidence Score badge, accompanied by supporting GO terms, Pfam domains, and homolog evidence. — **Reversibility:** reversible

### Engineering Hardening
- **D-16:** Strict UniProt & Sequence Validation — Enforce strict regex without commas (`^[OPQ][0-9][A-Z0-9]{3}[0-9]|[A-NR-Z][0-9]([A-Z][A-Z0-9]{2}[0-9]){1,2}$`) using `re.fullmatch()`. Explicitly handle non-canonical amino acids. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-17:** Strict Offline Mode & Robust Cache Keys — In `BaseBioClient`, if `offline_mode=True`, never attempt live HTTP fallback. Hash the entire sequence + query parameters for RCSB sequence search cache keys to prevent 32-residue collisions. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-18:** IntAct Client Hardening — Refactor IntAct queries to reuse the shared `requests.Session`, retry configuration, and timeout settings. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-19:** Canonical 1–6 Stage Architecture — Unify stage definitions across documentation, `quaternary/session.py`, and `Quaternary_Structure.ipynb`:
  - Stage 1: Sequence & Accession Discovery (UniProtKB & Complex Portal)
  - Stage 2: Experimental Assemblies & PDB Candidate Search (RCSB PDB & Entity Mapping)
  - Stage 3: Thermodynamic & Assembly Qualification (PDBe PISA interfaces & $\Delta G_{\text{diss}}$)
  - Stage 4: Homology Modeling & Physical Interactions (SWISS-MODEL & IntAct Partners)
  - Stage 5: Predictive Multimer Modeling (Real-Execution Only)
  - Stage 6: Biological Synthesis, Complex Disambiguation & Functional Inference
  Remove all confusing method aliases (`run_stage_3_pisa_interfaces`, `run_stage_4_swiss_model`, etc.). — **Reversibility:** costly
</decisions>
