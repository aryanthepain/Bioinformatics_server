## Quaternary-to-Function Inference Engine

### Mechanistic Archetype Mapping Strategy
| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Curated archetype rules | Rules for major symmetries (D7, C2, C3, C4, A6) combined with UniProt/GO/Pfam extraction | ✓ |

**User's choice:** Options 1 and 3 combined (Hybrid rule engine with pre-built structural-mechanistic archetypes plus an extensible registry).

**Notes:** Balances experimental evidence strength with thermodynamic interface stability.

### Structural Homolog & Partner Pathway Enrichment
| Option | Description | Selected |
